Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

GameFun Publisher ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the GameFun channel to publish their own videos to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission, the app receives an access token and basic account information (account ID and username) from TikTok and Instagram. It uses them only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to check publishing status.

Where data is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not collect data about any other people.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok and Instagram (Meta) through their official APIs.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok or Instagram settings and delete the local settings file. After that, the app keeps no data.
