GameFun Publisher

GameFun Publisher is a private desktop tool used by the creator of the GameFun channel to publish their own BeamNG.drive short videos to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

The tool runs only on the creator's own computer. It uploads videos the creator made, with the caption the creator wrote, at the time the creator scheduled. It is not offered to other users.
